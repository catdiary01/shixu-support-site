Two ways to see time

Plan by day. Time by second.

Choose the view that fits what you are tracking.

◷

Date countdowns

Keep birthdays, work milestones, and other dates in one place. See remaining calendar days, choose an event style, mark favorites, and archive records.

◴

Precise timers

Start with a duration or an end time and count down to the second. Manage multiple timers, with pause, resume, and repeat where supported.

▦

Home Screen widgets

Separate date and timer widgets make key information easy to glance at. iOS controls when widgets refresh, so updates may be delayed.
