Privacy Policy

Applies to Shixu 1.0.0 (9); effective November 15, 2026.

1. Scope and version

This policy applies to version 1.0.0 (9) of the Shixu iPhone app, including the app and its date and timer widgets. Shixu is operated by an individual independent developer, not a company or other registered business entity. Operator: 木槿（Independent Developer of Shixu）. Effective date: November 15, 2026.

2. Information you enter and local processing

You can create date countdowns and timers in the app. Records may contain names, target dates or times, notes, event styles, favorites, archive status, reminder choices, and timer state. Text you enter may contain personal information; you decide what to include.

You may also import audio for a custom timer sound. The app reads the selected file, checks its audio track and duration, and converts it into a file for local notifications. Its name and selection status are stored in local settings.

The app processes this information to display countdowns and timers, save your settings, schedule reminders you enable, and show widgets.

3. Local storage and widget sharing

The current app stores date and timer records and settings in its local UserDefaults. Converted custom sounds are stored in the app’s local Library/Sounds directory. The main app shares the summaries needed for display with its widgets through an App Group on the same device. Date summaries may include names, target dates, and favorite status; timer summaries may include names, start and end times, and running state. Widgets do not read the full record store directly.

The current app does not implement its own iCloud or other cross-device sync. Device-level system backups may include app data. System backup and app-managed sync are separate mechanisms; backup and restoration depend on your device and system settings.

4. Local notifications

If you enable a reminder and grant iOS notification permission, the app schedules a local notification through iOS. A date notification may use an event name as its title. A timer completion alert may include the timer name and use a locally selected sound. Turning off reminders or revoking permission affects future alerts. Processing by the device’s notification system does not mean the record is uploaded to a developer server.

5. Network services, third parties, and developer collection

This version does not offer user accounts, app-managed cloud sync, ads, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting, and it does not integrate third-party network SDKs. The app is not designed to send date or timer records to a developer server. iOS system backups and emails you choose to send to support are described separately in this policy.

6. Deletion, backup, and recovery

Regular deletion moves a record to Recently Deleted, where you can restore it. Permanent Delete is available there and removes the record from current records. Before each successful record change, the app writes the previous current records to a local recovery backup. After Permanent Delete, that backup may therefore still contain the deleted record. A later successful record change replaces the backup with the newer previous version. The current implementation has no timed or deletion-specific cleanup for this backup.

If the main records cannot be read and you confirm restoration from a valid backup, the app first stores the unreadable main data in a local quarantine copy. The current implementation has no automatic cleanup for this copy; it may retain historical content after later record changes or deletions.

Deleting a custom sound in the app’s settings first removes it from the list and marks its file for cleanup. After rebuilding local notifications, the app attempts to delete the file if no pending notification references it. If cleanup is incomplete or fails, the file may remain temporarily and be retried when notifications are rebuilt later. After record changes, the app requests an update to same-device widget summaries and rebuilds local notifications. Writes or system refreshes may be delayed or fail, so older summaries or pending notifications are not guaranteed to disappear immediately. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local device container, but system device backups may retain separate copies under iOS backup and retention settings. This policy does not promise immediate deletion of every copy.

7. Contacting support and privacy requests

The current app has no built-in feedback upload feature. The operator personally receives, reads, and handles support and privacy emails you choose to send, solely to answer questions, troubleshoot problems, and address privacy requests. These emails may contain your sender address, problem description, screenshots, and attachments that you provide. We do not ask you to send unnecessary sensitive personal information. Emails are not used for advertising and are not automatically forwarded to third-party AI or analytics services. After an issue is resolved, the developer deletes emails that are no longer needed as appropriate; records may be retained where applicable law requires it. There is no fixed 90-day retention period and no automated support, auto-reply, or automatic deletion system.

You can view, edit, and delete current records in the app and ask questions about data handling through the official privacy contact channel. Privacy contact email (shared with support): grippe-bearded9p@icloud.com. For device backups, also review your iOS backup settings.

8. Policy changes

When app features or data handling change, we will update this page and show the new effective date.
